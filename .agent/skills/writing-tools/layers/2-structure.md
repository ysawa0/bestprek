# Layer 2: Structure

Shape the whole piece: what stays, its order, and its ending.

Apply the Always section and each rule whose target appears.

Done when every applied rule here passes its Pass line.

## Always

### 10. Cut big, then small.

Requirement: Cut in this order: off-purpose sections, duplicated paragraphs, redundant sentences, expendable phrases, then expendable words. Preserve unique evidence, qualifications, required details, and steps the reader needs. Complete the structural cuts before line polishing.

Pass: Every remaining passage contributes a distinct claim, evidence, transition, characterization, or required action.

### 39. Write toward an ending.

Requirement: Choose the final answer, decision, action, resolution, or image before polishing. Make the preceding material prepare it. End immediately after delivering it. Place essential qualifications before the final emphasis and preserve their scope.

Pass: The ending completes the opening promise, introduces no unsupported claim, and has no trailing generic recap.

## When the target appears

### 19. Vary the lengths of paragraphs.

Target: A multi-section piece.

Requirement: Give each paragraph one topic or narrative beat. Start a new paragraph when that job changes. Put the central takeaway in a one-sentence paragraph and develop its evidence in surrounding paragraphs. In a one-paragraph format, preserve the format and mark the turn within it.

Pass: Paragraph breaks follow changes of job; the central takeaway is visibly distinguished without violating the requested format.

### 32. Place gold coins along the path.

Target: A multi-section piece.

Requirement: Give every substantive section a concrete reader payoff: a revealing fact, useful example, resolved question, actionable instruction, or new implication. Distribute those payoffs through the piece instead of saving all value for the ending.

Pass: Every substantive section delivers identifiable new value; none consists solely of setup or repetition.

### 33. Repeat, repeat, and repeat.

Target: A multi-section piece.

Requirement: Repeat the central term at the opening, a major transition, and the conclusion of a multi-section piece. Give the recurrence a new job each time: establish, develop, resolve. In shorter pieces, repeat only for reference or an explicit rhetorical pattern. Keep wording stable for the same concept.

Pass: Long-form recurrence follows establish–develop–resolve; every other repetition has a reference or pattern function.

### 37. In short works, don't waste a syllable.

Target: A short-form brief or a length limit.

Requirement: For a short-form brief, remove preambles, repeated context, redundant modifiers, and closing restatements. Give each word an informational, grammatical, rhythmic, or voice-preserving job. Meet the requested length without sacrificing necessary qualifications or unique facts.

Pass: The text meets its length limit, preserves the required meaning, and contains no removable setup or repetition.
