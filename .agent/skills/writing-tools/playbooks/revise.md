# Revise

Edit, tighten, shorten, or restyle supplied prose.

1. Lock the scope: what the user authorized, and what stays byte-identical. A narrow request, such as spelling only, ends here with that fix delivered.
2. Classify the piece (Rule 25) and map its existing structure. List the triggered rules whose targets appear.
3. Structure pass: cut big (Rule 10), then order the answer and the ending (Rules 25 and 39) and apply the structural triggered rules.
4. Paragraph pass: apply every listed rule whose target is a paragraph or its length.
5. Sentence and word pass: apply every always rule and every remaining listed rule. Qualifiers move into the middle of the sentence; they stay in the text.
6. Check every applied rule's Pass line. Done when each passes after its fix and a recheck of the rules that fix touched.
7. Deliver.
