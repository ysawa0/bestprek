# Revise

Edit, tighten, shorten, or restyle supplied prose.

1. Lock the scope. Name what the user authorized and what stays untouched: facts, quotations, qualifiers, and out-of-scope text. A narrow request, such as spelling only, ends here with that fix alone.
2. Classify the piece (Rule 25) and map its existing structure (Rule 24).
3. Structure pass. Cut big (Rule 10), order the answer and the ending (Rules 25 and 39), and apply the triggered structure rules.
4. Paragraph pass. Apply the triggered paragraph rules, such as 19, 33, and 18.
5. Sentence and word pass. Apply every always rule and every triggered sentence rule. Preserve factual qualifiers by moving them into the middle, not by deleting them.
6. Check each applied rule's Pass line. Fix every failure and recheck the requirements it touches.
7. Deliver the revised prose with only essential gap or conflict notes.
